# Local export archive retains completed exports for 14 days

Only workspace owners can access the archive. It excludes files that failed before upload.

The archive is separate from the recycle bin. Deleting an archived export does not delete the source records.

## Download an export in 4 steps

1. Step 1 of 4: Open **Workspace settings → Exports**.
2. Step 2 of 4: Select a completed export from the list.
3. Step 3 of 4: Check the date and row count. Then download the ZIP file.
4. Step 4 of 4: Keep the ZIP in an approved project folder. It may contain customer data.

## Large exports split into files; links expire

- Exports larger than 2 GB are split into numbered ZIP files.
- A link expires 30 minutes after creation.
- Workspace guests cannot view the archive.
- If the archive is empty, check the selected workspace before requesting a new export.
