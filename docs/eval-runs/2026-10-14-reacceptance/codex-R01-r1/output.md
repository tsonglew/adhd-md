# Retry a failed thumbnail job

Use these 4 steps to retry a failed thumbnail job. Do not retry jobs marked archived. Manual retry settings apply only to this browser session; signing in elsewhere does not copy those manual settings.

1. Step 1 of 4: Open the job panel. Then select a failed job.
2. Step 2 of 4: If the selected job's lock is active, wait 7 minutes. After that wait, reload the panel once.
3. Step 3 of 4: Copy the job key into the incident note.
4. Step 4 of 4: Submit the retry request.

Check the request status: a successful request shows accepted. Accepted does not mean the thumbnail has finished rendering.
