# Local export archive

The archive stores completed exports for 14 days. Only workspace owners can view it, and it excludes files that failed before upload.

Reading time: about 1 minute.

The archive is separate from the recycle bin. Deleting an export from the archive does not delete the source records.

## Download an export in 4 steps

1. Open **Workspace settings → Exports**.
2. Select a completed export from the list. Exports that failed before upload, or that are older than 14 days, do not appear in the list.
3. Check the date and row count before you download the ZIP file.
4. Keep the ZIP in an approved project folder; it may contain customer data.

## Limits: 2 GB splits, 30-minute links, owners only

- The archive splits exports larger than 2 GB into numbered ZIP files.
- A link expires 30 minutes after creation.
- Workspace guests cannot view the archive.
- If the archive is empty, check the selected workspace before you request a new export.
