# Retry a failed thumbnail job

Retry one failed thumbnail job in 4 steps.

Your manual retry settings apply only to this browser session. Signing in elsewhere does not copy those manual settings. Do not retry jobs marked archived.

1. Step 1 of 4: Open the job panel. Select a failed job.
2. Step 2 of 4: If the job's lock is active, wait 7 minutes. After the wait, reload the panel once.
3. Step 3 of 4: Copy the job key into the incident note.
4. Step 4 of 4: Submit the retry request. A successful request shows status accepted.

Status accepted does not mean the thumbnail has finished rendering.
